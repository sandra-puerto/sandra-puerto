<h1 id="-español">💫 Sobre mí</h1>

Desarrolladora backend con una trayectoria dual: operando y dirigiendo infraestructura real, y construyendo software — lo que me permite escribir código con conciencia de costo, riesgo y continuidad operativa, no solo de limpieza técnica.

Mi base principal es el diseño y modernización de backends (**PHP 8 / Laravel**) bajo estándares **Clean Code** y **PSR**. Sin embargo, complemento este desarrollo con un dominio profundo de la fase de despliegue y orquestación: desde infraestructura on-premise (Proxmox, Linux) hasta la automatización de arquitecturas multi-tenant en Docker protegidas por redes **Zero Trust** (Cloudflare Tunnel, Tailscale). 

Sumo a esto un conocimiento sólido en **Gobierno de TI e ITAM**: ciclo de vida, licenciamiento, planeación presupuestal y evaluación de inversión tecnológica (CAPEX/OPEX), priorizando el costo total de propiedad (TCO) sobre el costo inicial — lo que me permite desplegar plataformas robustas y altamente escalables incluso en entornos con recursos limitados.

- 💻 **Desarrollo & Automatización:** PHP 8 (Laravel, microframeworks propios), Python (desarrollo de CLIs y orquestadores), Clean Code y PSR.
- 🏗️ **Infraestructura & Contenedores:** Orquestación con Docker, virtualización on-premise (Proxmox), redes aisladas y alta disponibilidad.
- 🔐 **Seguridad Zero Trust:** Cloudflare Access/Tunnels, Tailscale, IAM y segmentación estricta de tráfico sin exponer puertos a internet.
- 📊 **Gobierno de TI:** ITAM, análisis CAPEX/OPEX y gestión del ciclo de vida de proveedores y adquisiciones.
- 🤖 **Ingeniería asistida por IA:** Uso estratégico de agentes multi-modelo para acelerar entregas y dominar nuevos stacks fuera de mi zona de confort.

<h1 id="-english">💫 About Me</h1>

Backend developer with a dual track record: operating and managing real-world infrastructure, and building software. This hybrid experience allows me to write code with a deep awareness of cost, security risk, and business continuity, not just technical cleanliness.

My core foundation is designing and modernizing backends (**PHP 8 / Laravel**) adhering strictly to **Clean Code** and **PSR** standards. However, I complement this with a deep mastery of the deployment and orchestration lifecycle: from on-premise infrastructure (Proxmox, Linux) to the automation of multi-tenant Docker architectures protected by **Zero Trust** networks (Cloudflare Tunnels, Tailscale).

I pair my technical skills with solid knowledge in **IT Governance and ITAM**: lifecycle management, licensing, budget planning, and CAPEX/OPEX analysis. I prioritize Total Cost of Ownership (TCO) over initial costs, allowing me to deploy robust, highly scalable platforms even in resource-constrained environments.

- 💻 **Development & Automation:** PHP 8 (Laravel, custom microframeworks), Python (CLI & Orchestrator development), Clean Code, and PSR.
- 🏗️ **Infrastructure & Containers:** Docker orchestration, on-premise virtualization (Proxmox), isolated networking, and high availability.
- 🔐 **Zero Trust Security:** Cloudflare Access/Tunnels, Tailscale, IAM, and strict traffic segmentation without exposing ports to the internet.
- 📊 **IT Governance:** ITAM, CAPEX/OPEX analysis, vendor lifecycle, and procurement management.
- 🤖 **AI-Assisted Engineering:** Strategic use of multi-model AI agents to accelerate delivery and master new tech stacks outside my primary comfort zone.

## 📌 Featured Project: Stratum Ecosystem
I designed and programmed a complete ecosystem to run multiple enterprise applications in an isolated, secure, and cost-effective manner on a single server:
- **[stratum-core](https://github.com/sandra-puerto/stratum-core):** Core Docker architecture featuring a shared DMZ network, a unified Layer 4 gateway, and unprivileged containers that expose zero local ports to the internet.
- **[stratum-cli](https://github.com/sandra-puerto/stratum-cli):** A Python orchestrator I built from scratch to automate multi-tenant provisioning, cryptographic password generation, database isolation, and backup management.
- **[overleaf-stratum](https://github.com/sandra-puerto/overleaf-stratum):** A production-grade implementation of Overleaf Community Edition injected into the Stratum ecosystem.
